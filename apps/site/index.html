Principles

  • Shape signals function.

    Pills act. Rounded boxes hold. You can tell what a thing does before you read it.

  • Depth from surface.

    No shadows, no gradients, no structural borders. Layers are fills, and fills are tokens.

  • Color is earned.

    Neutral first. When color shows up, it means something.
